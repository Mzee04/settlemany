## Slide 4 — The Story of Settlemany

**Title:** The Story of Settlemany

We wrote the weekend down in dollars: the apartment, dinner, the boat, suya.

The app crossed out the debts that already cancel and left three payments, all to one person.

Two Paytaca wallets on chipnet each signed only their own coins. One transaction paid the receiver 9,060 sats. Fee: 437 sats.

That was not a simulation. The tx is on chipnet.

**Title:** Benefits of Settlemany

- Dollars in. Exact BCH or PUSD out.
- Fewest payments. Integer cents. Same answer every time.
- One transaction for the whole group.
- Each person signs only their own inputs.
- If a wallet cannot share a transaction, they pay their share alone.
- Receipt with txid, explorer link, CSV, and PDF.

## Slide 6 — BCH Usage

**Title:** BCH Usage

Settlemany spends real P2PKH coins on Bitcoin Cash.

Every signature is SIGHASH_ALL plus FORKID plus UTXOS, so nobody can swap an output after the first person signs.

Creditors can be paid in PUSD, the ParyonUSD CashToken, one cent to one token unit. Or in BCH at a live price.

Wallets connect with WizardConnect. Chipnet for tests. Mainnet when it is real money.

Proof: https://explorer.selene.cash/tx/10976994b4ffab36a9f6eb8d25459ca3451e5d65302e867e1076dbd46a0f5927?network=chipnet
